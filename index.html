Privacy Policy

Pixel Space Dodge
Effective Date: September 4, 2026
Last Updated: September 4, 2026

Summary: Pixel Space Dodge is a fully offline game. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. No internet connection is required or used.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how RetroSpace ("we", "us", or "our") handles data in the Pixel Space Dodge mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. The App does not require or collect any personally identifiable information, including but not limited to:

  • Name, email address, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Photos, contacts, or files
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data

3. Data Storage

The App stores game progress data (scores, unlocked levels, settings, and achievements) locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data:

  • Remains entirely on your device
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Is automatically deleted when you uninstall the App

4. Internet and Network Access

The App does not request or use the INTERNET permission. It requires no network connectivity and does not communicate with any external servers, APIs, or services.

5. Permissions

The App requests the following Android permission:

  • VIBRATE — Used to provide haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g., collisions, power-up collection). This permission does not access any personal data.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services, including:

  • Advertising or ad networks
  • Analytics platforms
  • Social media SDKs
  • Crash reporting tools
  • Payment processors

7. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not collect any data from children or any other users. The App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.

8. Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access to your information. All game data remains stored locally on your device under your operating system's standard application sandbox security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
